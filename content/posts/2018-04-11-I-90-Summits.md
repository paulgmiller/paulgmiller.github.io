Seattle has a huge variety of hiking areas, but sometimes you need to crank one out and get back for the kids' bath time. So hooray for I-90.

**2026 update:** I am now trying to hike all the peaks between Bellevue and Snoqualmie Pass. Follow the project on my [I-90 Summits map and photo index](/posts/2026-09-04-i90-summits/).

## The hikes

### [Mount Si](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/mount-si)

Ah, the classic. Lunch on the rocks. Make sure you do the Haystack.

![Mount Rainier from Mount Si](/img/summits/si.JPG)

### [Mount Teneriffe](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/mount-teneriffe-road-trail)

Actually a better view than Si and pretty much the same drive. The hike is a bit longer, and the last stretch through the woods is a bit chewed up.

![View from Mount Teneriffe](/img/summits/teneriffe.jpg)

### [Rattlesnake Ledge](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/rattlesnake-ledge)

Take your grandparents; it's fast. I still need to go up to [East Peak](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/east-peak-rattlesnake-trail) or do the ten miles to [Snoqualmie Point](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/rattle-snake-mountain).

![Rattlesnake panorama](/img/summits/rattlesnake.jpg)

### [Mailbox Peak](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/mailbox-peak)

The new trail isn't the crazy mud cliff the old one was. More like Si, but 30 percent harder with 40 percent better views.

![Mailbox Peak panorama](/img/summits/mailbox.jpg)

### [Mount Washington](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/mount-washington-1)

An almost-secret entrance east of Twin Falls. Steep, gravelly trail and cool tree hallways. I really wanted the Chester Morse Lake view, but got fog.

![Fog at the Mount Washington summit](/img/summits/washington.jpg)

### [McClellan Butte](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/mcclellan-butte)

Quite the slog, but the rock scramble is phenomenal. Also a rare summit south of I-90.

![View from McClellan Butte](/img/summits/PXL_20220731_172742306.PANO.jpg)

### [Bandera Mountain](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/bandera-mountain)

Meadow scramble, but more of a ridge than a summit. Still: great flowers and Mount Rainier views.

![Bandera Mountain vertical panorama](/img/summits/bandera.jpg)

### [Mount Defiance](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/mount-defiance)

Secretly great. Turn left toward Bandera, pass Mason Lake, and keep going. The view feels nicely removed from the highway.

![Mount Defiance panorama](/img/summits/Defiance.jpg)

### [Granite Mountain](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/granite-mountain-1)

It had been eight years; I needed to go back. I love the alpine meadows, and I'm a sucker for a lookout.

![Granite Mountain lookout](/img/summits/granite.JPG)

### [Kendall Katwalk](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/kendall-katwalk)

Love this hike. It starts on the PCT. Pretty long, but once it gets pretty, it just stays that way for miles.

![Kendall Katwalk](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a1/Kendall_Katwalk_Trail.jpg)

### [Mount Catherine](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/mount-catherine)

Took a six-month-old up, including the nine-foot rock scramble with a chain at the top. Great view of Mount Rainier, but also bugs at the summit.

![Benji's first summit](/img/summits/catherine.jpg)

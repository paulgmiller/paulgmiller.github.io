From the neighborhood to the snow

Your Wednesday,
with a little altitude.

For third, fourth, and fifth graders. From first-time skiers to experienced kids, classes are placed by skill level and class size.

  1. 1:30 pm Load the bus

    Norwood Swim Club
    2340 123rd Pl SE, Bellevue
    Adult chaperones ride on the bus.

    2½ hours at Summit Central with Snow Sports Consulting Service (SSCS).

  3. About 6:45 pm Back at Norwood

    Head home after an afternoon on the mountain.

Save your seat

Three steps to the slopes.

  1. Register & sign

    Open SSCS’s On-snow Programs & Info page and choose Woodridge. Complete and sign the online form, then verify your email so it goes directly to SSCS.

    Read the full program form & refund policy (PDF)

  2. Pay the $700 tuition

    Your registration and payment secure your spot, first come, first served. SSCS accepts Zelle or check. They’ll email confirmation after receiving both.

    See payment details below.

  3. Get your season pass

    A season pass is required for every student and is purchased separately through the Summit at Snoqualmie. SSCS will email pass information after registration and payment. Purchase by December 15, 2026.

    Season ski rentals are also separate from tuition.

How to pay by Zelle or check

For Zelle, use contact name Snow Sports Consulting Service and email info@snowsportsconsultingservice.com or phone 206-406-0986. Include the participant’s name and “Woodridge” in the payment note.

For a check, contact SSCS promptly so they know it is on the way. Mail to: Snow Sports Consulting Service LLC, 204 165th Ave SE, Bellevue, WA 98008-4726.

Questions about Zelle enrollment? Contact SSCS for help.

Weather, absences & refunds

If weather cancels a trip, the program is extended by the number of trips missed. The form sets March 17, 2027 as the completion cutoff.

Missed days due to a participant’s absence do not qualify for makeups, refunds, or credits. Cancellation refund amounts depend on when a request is submitted. Please read the complete refund policy in the registration PDF and contact SSCS with questions.

Interested in chaperoning?

The registration form includes an option to volunteer as a chaperone for all six weeks. Contact Paul to learn more.

Questions? Get in touch.
